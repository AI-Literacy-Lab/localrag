ABCDEF Technologies - Employee Handbook (2026)
 
1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per year. Up to 5 unused days
can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted at
least 10 working days in advance through the Nimbus portal.
 
2. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST.
A one-time work-from-home stipend of $350 is provided for ergonomic equipment.
 
3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 22 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
receive 6 weeks. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.
 
4. Expense Reimbursement
Claims must be filed within 30 days. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to
$40 per day. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
 
5. Notice Period
The standard notice period is 60 days for all grades. Employees in Grade L5 and above
must serve 90 days.
